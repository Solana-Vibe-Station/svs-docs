# About Solana Vibe Station

Solana Vibe Station (SVS) is a self-hosted Solana RPC node provider built by engineers in Atlanta, Georgia. We don't resell cloud infrastructure—every server, every network cable, every piece of our platform is owned, operated, and maintained by us in our own datacenter.

## What We Offer

**RPC Services**
Multiple performance tiers from public endpoints to ultra-low-latency elite access, all backed by dedicated hardware in our Atlanta facility.

**Staked RPC / SWQoS**
Stake-weighted Quality of Service for guaranteed transaction prioritization and reserved bandwidth.

**Lightspeed Transactions**
Fast-track your transactions with tip-based priority, powered by our direct hardware integration.

**Geyser gRPC Streaming**
Real-time account and transaction stream subscriptions with ultra-low latency, IP-whitelist authenticated.

**VPS Hosting**
Dedicated virtual servers colocated in our Atlanta datacenter with direct network access.

**SVS API**
Proprietary token data and analytics API, custom-built for Solana ecosystem queries.

## Why We're Different

We built Solana Vibe Station because existing RPC providers rely on cloud infrastructure, third-party APIs, and resold compute. That introduces latency, dependency risks, and opacity. We took a different approach:

- **On-Premises Infrastructure**: No AWS, no cloud resellers. Everything runs in our own datacenter.
- **Hand-Built Hardware**: Direct-attach SFP+ cables, VLAN isolation, custom network topology designed for Solana.
- **Engineers Who Built It**: Founded by developers with deep expertise in electrical engineering, computer engineering, IT systems, and infrastructure.
- **Transparent Performance**: Live metrics every 5 minutes. You see what you get.
